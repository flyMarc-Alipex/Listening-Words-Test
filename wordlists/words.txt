# WordCard 词表 — 释义+例句版示例
# 格式:英文 <|> 释义①(中文 + 英文释义) <|> 例句① <|> 释义② <|> 例句② …
# 每个义项跟一条自己的例句,数量一一对应。空格翻面,← → 切换,R 随机。

example | ① 例子、示例 (example, instance) | For example, many people now work from home. | ② 榜样 (role model) | She set a good example for the team.
practice | ① 练习、实践 (exercise, training) | Regular practice is the key to improving your English. | ② 惯例、诊所、事务 (custom, medical practice) | It is normal practice to send a written follow-up.
memorize | 记忆、记住 (commit to memory) | I need to memorize these words before the test.
review | ① 复习、回顾 (revise, go over) | Let's review what we learned last week. | ② 评论、审查 (evaluation, inspection) | The film received good reviews.
recall | ① 回想、回忆 (remember, call to mind) | I can't recall her phone number. | ② 召回 (withdraw a product) | The company recalled thousands of cars last month.
fluent | 流利的、流畅的 (smooth; able to speak a language easily) | She is fluent in three languages.
vocabulary | 词汇、词汇量 (the words a person knows) | Reading widely builds your vocabulary.
pronounce | 发音、读作 (say or sound out a word) | How do you pronounce this word?
sentence | ① 句子 (a group of words forming a statement) | Write one sentence with each new word. | ② 判刑 (prison term) | He received a three-year prison sentence.
translate | 翻译 (convert from one language to another) | I used an app to translate the letter.
